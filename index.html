PROJECT 01

An end-to-end recruitment automation connecting candidate intake, experience-based routing, HR decisions, status tracking, and automated communication in one n8n workflow.

WORKFLOW INCLUDES

  • Candidate data collection through Google Forms
  • Automatic candidate processing and categorization
  • Experience-based data routing
  • Organized candidate records in Google Sheets
  • Real-time Telegram notifications for HR
  • SHORTLIST and REJECT decision handling

  • Candidate status and decision tracking

SOLUTION

The workflow connects these steps in n8n so information moves automatically between tools while HR keeps control of shortlist and reject decisions.
